# Jean-Claude Otieno Ochieng — Portfolio

> A living record of my development as a Computer Science student and aspiring technology professional.

## About This Portfolio

This repository contains the source code for my personal portfolio website.

The portfolio is designed to represent more than a traditional résumé. It documents my professional growth, the things I build, the skills I develop, the projects I complete, and the direction I am pursuing in technology.

I am currently pursuing a **Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at Multimedia University of Kenya**.

My primary career direction is the development of strong, practical expertise across:

* **Networking**
* **Cybersecurity**
* **Cloud Computing**

A major part of my long-term goal is understanding how these areas connect and how they can be applied together to design, build, secure, and operate modern computing systems.

## Portfolio Philosophy

The portfolio follows a simple principle:

> **Learn. Build. Connect. Secure. Evolve.**

Rather than presenting only completed achievements, the portfolio will document meaningful stages of development over time.

Projects, certifications, technical experiments, laboratories, milestones, and other evidence of growth will gradually become part of the portfolio.

## What This Website Will Showcase

### Journey

## Development Approach

The portfolio is being developed incrementally.

Major development stages are tracked through Git commits so that the repository itself preserves part of the project's development history.

The general workflow is:

```text
Plan
  ↓
Build
  ↓
Test
  ↓
Refine
  ↓
Commit
  ↓
Push
  ↓
Deploy
```
